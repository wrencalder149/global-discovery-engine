World Reader desk — daily protocol

PACKS
1) briefing = 簡報. Short is allowed. 3 to 5 items. Each item 200 to 400 Chinese characters. Context required. No headline stack.
2) feature = 正式報導. NOT a teaser. 1 or 2 COMPLETE articles. Each 1200 to 2800 Chinese characters.
3) culture = 正式報導或評論. 1 or 2 COMPLETE pieces. Same length. No Marvel, box office, idol promo.

A COMPLETE ARTICLE MUST CONTAIN
- opening that states what is happening and why it matters now
- the object or conflict in concrete terms (who, where, number, institution)
- background that stops the reader from receiving a naked event
- what is confirmed, by which sources
- what is still single-source or disputed
- ending that does not invent a moral
If a piece is only a lead plus 「目前僅見 XX」, it is a briefing item. Do not put it in feature or culture.

METHOD
Collect many titles. Deep-read 8 to 12 full texts. Take notes in original language first. Then write Traditional Chinese Taiwan.
Paywall = clue, not fake article.
Hard facts only: deaths, loan vs repatriation, institution names, numbers.

OUTPUT FILES
desk/editions/YYYY-MM-DD/briefing.md
desk/editions/YYYY-MM-DD/feature.md
desk/editions/YYYY-MM-DD/culture.md
desk/editions/YYYY-MM-DD/notes.md
Then rebuild desk/feed.xml and commit main.

FAILURE RULE
If feature or culture is still a briefing, do not publish those packs.
