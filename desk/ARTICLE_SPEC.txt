What a complete readable article is

Not: title + 80 words + source line.
Not: the first paragraph of a wire.
Yes: a piece a person can sit down and finish, and know more at the end than at the start.

Minimum bones
1. What happened, in the first short block.
2. The scene or the mechanism (a museum room, a planning law, a glacier as a savings account).
3. Time depth: when it left, when it returned, which year the plan started.
4. Numbers with their nature: official, estimate, disputed.
5. At least one named institution or person.
6. The disagreement, if any.
7. A last paragraph that states the limit of what we know.

Deep pack shape
- One main reported article (the day’s center of gravity).
- Optionally a second shorter reported article, still complete.
- A labeled 「入口」 is allowed only when the full text was not readable; never disguise an entrance as a finished story.

Culture pack shape
- Same as deep, but the object may be an idea, a restoration, a book, a film print, a design argument.
- Still reported, not a list of album titles.
